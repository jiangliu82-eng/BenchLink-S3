# BenchLink-S3 wiring guide for the WDA-S3 development board

[简体中文](WDA-S3-devboard-wiring.md) · **English**

This guide covers the ESP32S3-COREBOARD. Its sources are page 1 of the manufacturer’s ESP32-S3-SCH.pdf, firmware/Kconfig.projbuild, WdaPairing.cpp and usb_cycle.c. Check the actual module capacity, fitted jumpers and target board on your unit.

1. **Maintenance port.** Connect the PC to USB1 (CH343P). The board routes UART0 to TX43 and RX44 at 115200 8N1. Verify that the module has at least 8 MB of flash and octal PSRAM. From the repository root, run `pwsh -File ./Flash-S3.ps1 -Python <esptool-interpreter-path> -Port COM5` with the actual port. Run `./Start-Wda.ps1 -OpenBrowser` to pair over the 2.4 GHz LAN.
2. **SWD.** Power the target independently, use 3.3 V logic and connect both grounds. Wire J1-17 / GPIO11 to SWDIO, J1-18 / GPIO12 to SWCLK, J1-19 / GPIO13 to active-low nRESET (pull-up on the target) and J1-22 to GND. Ensure the onboard debugger and WDA-S3 never drive the SWD signals simultaneously. Match the SWD test configuration to the chip and choose RAM reserved for safe test writes.
3. **USB2 data and power.** D− connects directly to GPIO19 and D+ to GPIO20. The USB-OTG jumper (0 Ω / 0603) is in parallel with D2. Closing it feeds the board’s 5 V rail back to USB2 VBUS. It can remain open for flashing, pairing or SWD alone. With power and cables removed, check both directions using resistance or diode mode on a multimeter. Identify USB-OTG carefully before soldering. Do not bridge IN-OUT or USB-JTAG by mistake.
4. **Bus-powered USB peripheral.** With power removed, close USB-OTG. Supply power through USB1 and verify that USB2 VBUS measures close to 5 V relative to ground. CC1 and CC2 each have a 5.1 kΩ pull-down, so the board does not advertise a Type-C source. For an initial test, use a passive USB-C male to USB-A female data adapter and a low-power, full-speed USB-A serial TTL adapter. Short the adapter’s own TX and RX for loopback. **With USB-OTG closed, never connect USB2 to a PC, charger or any port that supplies 5 V.** The board has no controlled VBUS current-limit switch. Restarting the root port tests re-enumeration only and cannot verify a physical power cycle.
5. **Target serial.** UART0 is for maintenance and pairing. To test the target UART using the external USB-to-TTL adapter, use 3.3 V logic, connect TX to RX, RX to TX and GND to GND, power both sides separately and leave VCC disconnected. The production board’s GPIO15/16 target UART is unavailable in this development-board implementation.
